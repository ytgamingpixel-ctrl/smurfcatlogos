    { Questions }

    Before you ask.

    How long does it take?

    It depends on the design and how much I have on at the time. Message me with what you need and I’ll give you a rough idea before you commit to anything.

    How do I pay?

    By bank transfer. We agree a price on Discord first, then I’ll send you my details — payment goes through before I start on it.

    What do I actually receive?

    An image file you can use straight away — as a server icon, a Discord avatar, or on your site. If you need a specific size or file type, mention it when we talk and I’ll see what I can do.

    Can I use it for my server?

    Yes — once it’s paid for it’s yours to use for your server, store and socials. I like to show work in my portfolio, so let me know if you’d rather I didn’t.

    What if it’s not what I wanted?

    Tell me what’s not working and I’ll look at changing it. The best way to avoid that is sending references and detail up front, so we start off pointed in the right direction.

    Can you match a style I’ve seen?

    Send references and I’ll tell you whether it’s something I can do. I won’t copy another designer’s logo, but I can work in a similar direction.
